chainboot reader

Read a system from the chain, right here

This page finds a publisher's catalog on Solana, downloads an image and checks every byte: the catalog by the publisher's signature, everything else by SHA-256. It talks only to the RPC endpoints named below. Nothing is installed and nothing leaves your browser.

1. Where to read

The public devnet endpoint is free and slow: a small system takes about half a minute. An endpoint of your own (many providers have a free plan) is much faster; with several, the reader also asks more than one which catalog is the newest.

2. The publisher's images

Choose one to download and verify it. Start with a small one.
